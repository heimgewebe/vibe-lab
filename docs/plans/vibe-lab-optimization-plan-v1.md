# Vibe-Lab Optimization Plan v1

## Decision

Vibe-Lab is narrowed to a small experiment and decision surface. It may register a prospective comparison, validate evidence identity, evaluate a bounded effect, and close with a reviewed decision. It must not become a scheduler, dashboard, runtime service, routing authority, second Bureau, or second Grabowski governor.

## Verified starting point

At plan registration, the repository contained 24 experiment directories, 36 Operator-Lab run cards, 114 validator-adjacent files, and only one adoption decision. The Operator-Lab series still used a three-observation decision from 2026-07-01 despite 36 run cards. This imbalance is the first optimization target.

## Phases

1. **Close Operator-Lab.** Produce a deterministic cross-run assessment, expose missing metadata and timing, replace the stale decision, and freeze new anecdotal cards.
2. **Make active work explicit.** Keep at most five active experiments in one validated registry. Historical experiments remain readable but do not become active by implication.
3. **Introduce one prospective comparison contract.** Require consumer, decision target, intervention, control, treatment, primary metric, minimum material effect, comparability constraints, confounders, review date, expiry, and closure outcomes.
4. **Build one deterministic effect evaluator.** Report effect size, missingness, comparability, uncertainty, non-claims, and one of `beneficial`, `no_material_effect`, `harmful`, or `insufficient_evidence`.
5. **Consolidate validation.** Retain only validators with an active consumer and a named failure class; separate core, active-experiment, and legacy validation.
6. **Run real pilots.** Use naturally occurring Grabowski/Vibe-Lab work. Synthetic duplication and retrospective metric invention are forbidden.
7. **Decide.** Promote, pilot, defer, reject, or archive the evaluator after real use.

## Stop rules

Stop or remove the optimization slice if it requires a new service, database, dashboard, LLM-based pattern detector, automatic Bureau mutation, automatic routing changes, more specialist validators than it removes, or if three real pilots do not improve an operator decision.

## Organ boundaries

- Grabowski owns execution and typed receipts.
- Chronik may own append-only history when longitudinal storage is needed.
- Vibe-Lab owns prospective experiment design and reviewed effect evaluation.
- Bureau owns task and promotion decisions.
- Heimlern remains frozen; reusable validation ideas may be copied without a runtime dependency.

## Delivery slices

- Slice A: Operator-Lab closeout, active registry, plan registration.
- Slice B: prospective comparison contract and effect evaluator.
- Slice C: validator inventory and CI consolidation.
- Slice D: real pilots and final usefulness decision.
