# Operator-Lab series freeze

The historical Operator-Lab series is frozen after the 2026-07-12 cross-run closeout.

No new anecdotal run card may be appended to this series. A future operator experiment must use a prospective registration with a named consumer, decision target, control, treatment, primary metric, minimum material effect, review date, expiry, and closure outcome.

Existing artifacts remain immutable historical evidence. Missing run metadata and unmeasured timing are not reconstructed.
