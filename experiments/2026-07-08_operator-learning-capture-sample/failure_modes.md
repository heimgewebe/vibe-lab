Mitigation: keep this slice to a manual sample. Do not add validators, imports, or dashboards until the sample proves a missing contract.

## FM-2: Selection bias

Mitigation: treat all findings as candidate patterns. Do not claim representativeness.

RepoBrief, Cabinet, or PR bodies may be treated as proof rather than context or self-report.

Mitigation: every item records an evidence boundary and non-claims.

Mitigation: next slice is limited to a tiny friction-event contract, and only if this sample remains useful after review.
