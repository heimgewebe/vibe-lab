# Failure modes

- Retrospective metrics manufacture a favorable result.
- Repeated labels are mistaken for comparable tasks.
- Missing observations are interpreted as zero.
- A normal approximation is treated as causal proof.
- The evaluator duplicates Grabowski routing logic.
- A report is applied automatically or creates a Bureau task.
- Synthetic observations are used to satisfy the real-pilot threshold.
- Registration changes after observations are collected.
- One evidence artifact is counted under multiple references.
- Concurrent writers silently lose an observation.
- An observation is captured after the registered expiry.
- One person makes both paired decisions or scores their own decision.
- A free numeric score bypasses the registered component rubric.
- The scorer sees condition labels or decision order is not randomized.
- Review effort is estimated retrospectively instead of measured.
- A favorable primary effect is mistaken for acceptable cost.
