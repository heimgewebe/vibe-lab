# P1 activation receipt

P1 protocol revision 1 is frozen in `cohort-protocol.v1.yml`. This receipt
activates only prospective preparation for six natural full-form shadow cases.
It admits **0/6 cases**, contains no case identities and supplies **zero outcome
evidence**. No efficacy run has occurred.

The six vacant slots are `P1-01` through `P1-06` in strict order. A slot can be
filled only by an independently occurring eligible canonical Bureau intake,
using a separate prospective create-only admission record, after a complete full
outcome-case spec and digest have been frozen and before productive mutation.
Synthetic, retrospective, selected or backfilled cases are forbidden.

P1 records usability and observability measures only: form handling time,
unclear or redundant fields, pre-mutation changes identified for scope,
intervention or verifier, effect-observation state, authority violations,
technical-versus-domain divergence and whether an additional actionable finding
occurred. Because P1 contains no minimal-form comparator, none of these measures
establishes or implies minimal-versus-full efficacy and none counts toward P3.

The activation and every future P1 artifact have zero authority over task
selection, execution, routing, queues, policy, merge, deployment, technical
closeout or productive integration. The stop rules and authority boundaries are
frozen in the cohort protocol.

The eventual closeout is a distinct artifact at `p1/closeout.md`; it does not
exist at activation. It must be independently reviewed and must preserve the P1
non-claim. Even that closeout does not execute or authorize P3: P3 additionally
requires a separate execution authorization.
