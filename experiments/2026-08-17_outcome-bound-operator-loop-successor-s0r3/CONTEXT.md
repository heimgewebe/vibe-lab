# Context

S0-R2 was correctly rejected. Its independent review found two defects that must
not be repaired in place:

1. **Effect-lineage ambiguity:** the phrase "material sub-effect" did not force a
   unique boundary between a productive prerequisite and a prior act that already
   realised part of the later claimed effect. In create-then-mutate shapes such as
   R2-02/R2-08, both `eligible` and `indeterminate` remained defensible.
2. **Registration/procedure mismatch:** the registration promised eight paired
   S0/R2 observations although rejected S0 had six fixed cases while the R2 review
   classified eight R2-only cases.

S0-R3 is a separate paper revision. It removes `material sub-effect` from the
admission test instead of adding another ontology layer. For each case the author must freeze one pre-intervention **claim snapshot C**, one
target subject, its contemporaneous baseline, one independently falsifiable
**target-effect predicate E**, the minimal ordered **transition path T** from that
baseline to E, and non-outcome delivery qualifiers before classifying prior work.

Earlier productive work is lineage-positive only if it already made the frozen E
true, or already executed the frozen T such that the later proposal mainly changes
delivery qualifiers or packaging. Work that merely establishes the declared
baseline or another enabling input is antecedent. If C, subject, baseline, E, T or subject-equivalence cannot be bound from the
frozen evidence, the result is
`indeterminate`.

The experiment is paper-only. It may freeze the rule and adversarial cases and
obtain an independent exact-head semantic review. It may not reopen P1, start a
natural cohort, add a validator, or change Bureau, Grabowski runtime/routing,
Chronik, Leitstand, queue, policy, deployment, merge policy or any production
authority.
