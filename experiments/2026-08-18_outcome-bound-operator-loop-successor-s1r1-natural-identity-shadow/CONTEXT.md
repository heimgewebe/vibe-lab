# Context

## Why S1-R1 exists

S1 terminated with `REJECT_THIS_REVISION` because its frozen protocol did not uniquely define whether repeated post-activation supersession events for one pre-existing Bureau candidate identity were separate natural `intakes/arrivals`. The independent exact-head review correctly refused both the event-arrival and the later identity-first interpretation because choosing between them after arrivals would complete the sampling contract retrospectively.

S1-R1 is a new revision, not a repair of S1. It tests one narrower proposition: whether the already paper-reviewed S0-R3 C/S/B/E/T/Q admission semantics can be applied prospectively when the natural sampling unit is frozen *before activation* as one newly appearing canonical Bureau candidate identity.

## Scope boundary

S1-R1 changes only the sampling contract. S0-R3 semantics remain unchanged. It creates no validator, no new service, no Bureau schema or task-status field, no Grabowski runtime/routing effect, no Chronik or Leitstand integration, no P1 reopening, no P2/P3, and no Minimal-versus-Full comparison.

A successful pre-activation review can authorize only activation of this three-identity shadow. A later natural-sample PASS could justify a separate Decision-Impact proposal; it cannot itself authorize efficacy, adoption, routing or production integration.
