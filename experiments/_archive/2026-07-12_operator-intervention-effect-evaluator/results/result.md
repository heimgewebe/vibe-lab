# Result

The experiment was **not executed**. No real, synthetic or retrospective observation was captured.

The registered treatment decision was supposed to consume a deterministic evaluator report. The implemented report, however, consumes the already captured and independently scored control and treatment decisions. It therefore cannot be available before the treatment decision without adding a different pre-decision evaluator. That would change the intervention after registration rather than test the merged implementation.

The practical salvage path is rejected. The generic deterministic capture and effect-calculation utilities remain in Vibe-Lab as unpromoted review tooling; this archive establishes neither usefulness nor a reason to retain Heimlern as a separate evaluator, routing layer or runtime service.

See `practical-blockade.md` and `practical-blockade.v1.json` for the bound evidence and non-claims.
