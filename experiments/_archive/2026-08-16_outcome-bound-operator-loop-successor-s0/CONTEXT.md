# Context

## Why this successor exists

Outcome-Bound Operator Loop P1 revision 1 terminated early because its frozen
prospective rule prohibited any prior `productive mutation`. P1-02 then exposed a
semantic ambiguity: antecedent production state needed to expose a later gap was
indistinguishable, in the protocol text, from productive execution of the concrete
intervention whose outcome was to be assessed.

This successor does **not** repair or reinterpret P1 revision 1. P1 remains closed
exactly as recorded in its closeout. The successor asks a narrower paper-test
question: can prospective admission be defined relative to the concrete
intervention-under-test and its first productive effect without allowing
retrospective relabeling?

## Scope

S0 is a retrospective calibration exercise over six fixed paper cases. It may:

- define `situation_exists`, `intervention_identified` and
  `intervention_effect_begins` as separate boundaries;
- classify the six fixed calibration cases under that successor rule;
- compare those classifications with the broad P1 revision-1 rule;
- receive independent semantic review.

S0 may **not** admit a natural successor cohort, reopen P1, create or change Bureau
tasks, alter Grabowski runtime or routing, add a validator, change Chronik or
Leitstand, deploy anything, or authorize S1, S2, P2 or P3.

## Epistemic boundary

A successful S0 can establish only that the successor admission rule produced no
material classification disagreement on the six fixed calibration cases under an
independent exact-head semantic review. It cannot establish Outcome Case efficacy,
real-world handling overhead, false-completion prevention, Minimal-versus-Full
superiority, or suitability for productive integration.
