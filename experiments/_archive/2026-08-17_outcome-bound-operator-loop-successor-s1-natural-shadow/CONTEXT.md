# Context

## Why this successor exists

The original Outcome-Bound P1 cohort was stopped prospectively after P1-02 showed
that productive mutation had already begun before the required Stage A capture.
That result did not establish Outcome Case efficacy and did not authorize a retry,
replacement or backfill.

The first two paper successors were then rejected under independent review:

- S0 / PR #340: admission ambiguity and a residual/qualifier renaming escape;
- S0-R2 / PR #342: effect-lineage ambiguity plus registration/procedure mismatch.

S0-R3 / PR #344 passed its paper semantic gate on exact main commit
`66508a215e445c0759826f6c33b14b491dd61960`: eight of eight treatment
classifications were independently reproduced with zero material disagreement and
zero credible renaming escape.

That result establishes paper-level semantic stability only. It explicitly does
not establish natural-case reliability, decision impact, Outcome Case efficacy,
adoption readiness or production authority.

## Question for S1

Can the unchanged S0-R3 C/S/B/E/T/Q rule be applied prospectively to a very small,
non-selected natural sample quickly enough and reproducibly enough to justify any
later efficacy proposal?

S1 tests only this external-validity / handling question.

## Hard boundary

S1 does not reopen P1. It creates no validator and no Bureau, Grabowski runtime,
Chronik, Leitstand, routing, queue, policy, merge-policy or deployment integration.
It may not delay, alter, select, reprioritize or manufacture productive work.

The experiment may reference authoritative task and technical evidence; it may not
copy that evidence into a second technical truth.
